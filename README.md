Shooter Nerd is an independent game project developed by **zyuruEntertainment**. It features team-based combat, AI-controlled bots, weapon systems, and mobile-friendly gameplay.

## Features

- **Team-Based Combat** — Fight alongside your team against opposing players or AI opponents.
- **AI Bots** — Computer-controlled enemies with perception, movement, and combat behaviors.
- **Weapon System** — Shooting, reloading, and ammunition management.
- **Weapon Shop** — Purchase weapons and ammunition.
- **Mobile Controls** — Touch-based controls designed for Android devices.
- **Match System** — Health management, kill tracking, and victory conditions.
